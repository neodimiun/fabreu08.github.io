Profile

I am a Materials & Process Technology (M&PT) Engineer at Collins Aerospace Landing Systems, an RTX business. This role is a new path in my career: taking a decade of regulated laboratory science into aerospace materials, special processes, and process engineering.

My work now sits at the intersection of chemistry, process control, and airworthiness. I support the special processes that protect landing gear in manufacturing, overhaul, and repair — coatings and finishes, chemical processing, process qualification, and NADCAP-governed process control — with the same discipline I built in high-volume, audit-ready laboratories.

That foundation was built across environmental enforcement, pharmaceutical validation, and aerospace manufacturing. From wastewater analysis for municipal clients to disinfectant validation studies and environmental monitoring of ISO 4 cleanrooms in sterile drug manufacturing, my approach has stayed consistent: data integrity, traceability, and defensibility. I now apply that methodology to process control, shop-floor problem solving, and the materials technology behind mission-critical landing systems.
